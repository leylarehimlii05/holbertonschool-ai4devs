# Product Idea: CarpoolConnect

## Vision
Enable corporate commuters to share daily rides safely and efficiently, reducing commuting costs, traffic congestion, and carbon emissions while fostering workplace connections.

## Users
- **Corporate Drivers (Employees with Vehicles)**: Employees commuting to the same office or business park who want to fill empty seats, split fuel and parking costs, and earn company sustainability incentives.
- **Corporate Passengers (Employees without Vehicles)**: Employees seeking affordable, reliable, and stress-free daily transportation with verified coworkers along similar routes.
- **HR & Sustainability Managers (Company Admins)**: Organizational leaders who need to track employee commute emissions (ESG reporting), manage corporate parking capacity, and incentivize eco-friendly commuting habits.

## Key Features
- **Verified Corporate Matching**: Real-time route and schedule matching restricted exclusively to verified employees of the same company or partner business park via corporate SSO/email authentication.
- **Automated Cost-Splitting & Incentives**: Transparent fuel/toll cost calculation with seamless in-app micro-payments and integration with corporate commuter benefits or reward points.
- **Recurring Commute Scheduling**: Smart calendar sync to automate recurring weekday ride matches and handle last-minute schedule changes gracefully.
- **Trust, Safety & Ratings**: Mutual peer-to-peer rating system, live trip sharing, and verified employee badges to ensure comfort and accountability.
- **ESG & Eco-Impact Dashboard**: Personal and company-wide analytics tracking CO₂ emissions saved, total shared miles, and financial savings.
